Stories

Interactive narrative evidence-communication pieces built around historical clinical-trial reversals.

  • When Certainty Kills — The CAST Story

    A single-file interactive narrative on the Cardiac Arrhythmia Suppression Trial, where anti-arrhythmic drugs that suppressed premature ventricular contractions increased mortality. Predict outcomes at each decision point before the results are revealed.

  • Evidence Reversal 40

    A multi-story interactive app (40 evidence reversals) plus a self-contained meta-analysis engine: effect sizes, tau-squared estimators, heterogeneity, fixed- and random-effects pooling, prediction intervals, and publication-bias tests.

  • E156 Submission

    The E156 micro-paper submission artifacts for this project.
